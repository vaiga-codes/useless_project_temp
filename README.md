### Project Description
[RAGE.EXE is a fun, useless web app that analyzes your typing behavior to determine just how angry you are at your keyboard. It tracks typing speed, accuracy, backspaces, and other suspicious behavior to generate a completely unscientific “rage score” and diagnosis.]

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
[How do we scientifically determine whether someone is angry at their keyboard?]

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
[We monitor your typing like an unnecessarily concerned keyboard therapist. By tracking WPM, accuracy, backspaces, Caps Lock abuse, and rapid-fire keystrokes, we calculate a totally scientific™ Rage Score and diagnose whether you're calmly typing, mildly annoyed, or one keystroke away from sending the keyboard into witness protection.]
